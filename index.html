About Bluefox

Coordinated cleaning for construction projects and commercial properties.

Bluefox Cleaning Solutions is an in-house crew: our own trained people and our own specialized equipment on every job. We handle post-construction cleaning, commercial janitorial maintenance, pressure washing, window cleaning and debris removal, from small residential projects to large commercial buildings that call for a white-glove finish.

We work around the schedule as it shifts, and the result comes first, whatever it takes. We take pride in every project we undertake, and we work with the client on every specific need.

  • Professional

    Clear communication and a consistent standard of work on every scope.

  • Responsive

    Direct answers on scope, access, and scheduling when your team needs them.

  • Flexible

    Our plan adjusts as site readiness, access, and completion dates shift.

  • Detail-focused

    Surfaces, fixtures, and finishes are detailed ahead of your walkthrough.

Why Bluefox

Flexible enough for the jobsite. Professional enough for the final walkthrough.

One dependable partner for post-construction cleaning and ongoing commercial cleaning needs.

  • One-stop-shop convenience

    Multiple cleaning services are coordinated through one provider, so your team is not scheduling, briefing, and managing several separate vendors.

  • Flexibility for busy general contractors

    Construction schedules, site readiness, access, and completion dates change. We coordinate with your project team and adapt to what the project requires.

  • White-glove presentation

    Our goal is a space that is clean, detailed, and professionally presented for walkthroughs, client inspections, handover, and occupancy.

  • Professional and insured

    Bluefox Cleaning Solutions is fully insured: general liability, umbrella and workers' compensation, with a certificate of insurance on request.
